Research

Understanding how learning systems adapt, stabilize, and compute

My work begins with theoretical questions in reinforcement learning and game dynamics, then asks whether the resulting structure can support more reliable and efficient algorithms.

I am especially drawn to simple mathematical objects that connect different areas: an entropy coefficient can become a control variable for tracking drift, softmax geometry can determine global stability, and the computation path itself can become learnable.

Research threads

The common goal is to turn structural understanding into more predictable learning behavior.

01

Learning Dynamics

How can learning systems remain stable under feedback, strategic interaction, and environmental drift?

Entropy-regularized RL, non-stationary learning, logit responses, and game dynamics.

Tracking Drift · Sharp Spectral Thresholds

02

Adaptive Computation

How should a model allocate computation according to the structure and difficulty of each input?

Trainable computation paths, efficient inference, and internal routing in language models.

03

Reliable Learning

What observable structure tells us when a learning procedure is stable and trustworthy?

Spectral coverage, prompt-length selection, and stability under resampling.
