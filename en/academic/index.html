FBS: Modeling Native Parallel Reading inside a Transformer

Tongxi Wang · Sole author

Fovea-Block-Skip Transformer moves LLM acceleration from external decoding tricks to internal adaptive compute.

2026 · ICML 2026

Tracking Drift: Variation-Aware Entropy Scheduling for Non-Stationary Reinforcement Learning

Accepted

Tongxi Wang, Zhuoyang Xia, Xinran Chen, Shan Liu · First author

Adaptive Entropy Scheduling turns the entropy coefficient in non-stationary RL into a drift-responsive control signal.

  • Interprets entropy coefficients as tracking-control variables rather than fixed hyperparameters.
  • Derives a tracking-stability trade-off from dynamic mirror descent and non-stationary OCO.
  • Uses TD-error to build a critic-based drift proxy that can be integrated into PPO, SAC, and related algorithms.

2026 · arXiv preprint / under review

Sharp Spectral Thresholds for Logit Fixed Points

Under Review

Tongxi Wang · Sole author

A sharp spectral condition for uniqueness and global convergence in softmax self-feedback systems.

  • Studies softmax closed-loop feedback structures arising in entropy-regularized policy updates and multi-agent logit responses.
  • Shows that classical criteria are conservative because they ignore payoff-shift invariance and simplex tangent geometry.
  • Proves a sharp effective-direction condition based on tangent-projected softmax geometry.

2026 · ICASSP 2026

Via Score to Performance: Efficient Human-Controllable Long Song Generation with Bar-Level Symbolic Notation

Accepted

Tongxi Wang, Yang Yu, Qing Wang, Junlang Qian · First author

A score-to-performance pipeline for controllable long-song generation using editable bar-level symbolic notation.

  • Decouples composition, structure control, and audio rendering through an intermediate score representation.
  • Builds a prompt to lyrics/style to symbolic score to vocal/accompaniment rendering pipeline.
  • Improves controllability, editability, and lyric-music alignment in long-form song generation.

A spectral-coverage perspective on stable in-context learning under demonstration resampling.
