I am an undergraduate researcher at Southeast University, working on reinforcement learning theory, multi-agent systems, and efficient language models. I am especially interested in learning problems where mathematical structure leads to practical systems.

Research Focus

From theory RL to efficient language models, the work centers on learning systems that are principled, reliable, and practical.

Reinforcement Learning Theory

Entropy-regularized RL, non-stationary RL, dynamic regret, and adaptive exploration.

Multi-agent Systems and Game Dynamics

Logit fixed points, softmax response systems, potential games, and learning dynamics.

Efficient Large Language Models

Fovea-Block-Skip Transformer, inference efficiency, architecture, and routing.

Reliable In-Context Learning

Prompt-length selection, spectral coverage, and stability under resampling.
