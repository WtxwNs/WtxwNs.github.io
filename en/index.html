Research agenda

Three questions that connect my work

The applications change; the recurring questions concern feedback, computation, and reliability.

01

Learning Dynamics

How can learning systems remain stable under feedback, strategic interaction, and environmental drift?

Entropy-regularized RL, non-stationary learning, logit responses, and game dynamics.

Tracking Drift · Sharp Spectral Thresholds

02

Adaptive Computation

How should a model allocate computation according to the structure and difficulty of each input?

Trainable computation paths, efficient inference, and internal routing in language models.

Fovea-Block-Skip Transformer

03

Reliable Learning

What observable structure tells us when a learning procedure is stable and trustworthy?

Spectral coverage, prompt-length selection, and stability under resampling.

Stability of In-Context Learning
